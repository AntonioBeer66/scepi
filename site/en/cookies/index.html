Cookies and trackers

Last updated: October 11, 2026

No optional trackers

SCEP Invaders currently uses no advertising cookie, no audience-measurement cookie and no tracking pixel. No consent is therefore requested when you open the site.

Necessary trackers

The coinche game may use the browser’s local storage to resume a game. This is not an advertising cookie. The host may also set a technical security tracker needed to deliver and protect the service.

Changes to the site

If an analytics, video or social network service, or any other tracker subject to consent, is added, it will be loaded after a free and informed choice, with a button to refuse as easily as to accept. This page will be updated.

For any question: scep.invaders.escp@gmail.com.
