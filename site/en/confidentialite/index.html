Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

SCEP Invaders applies the principle of data minimization: the site collects only the data needed for its services and does not sell any personal data.

Data controller

SCEP Invaders, association under the French law of 1901, RNA W751228600. Contact: scep.invaders.escp@gmail.com.

Data and purposes

  • Contact: the email address and the content of a message are used only to answer the request.
  • Online coinche: nickname, seat, game data and an encrypted fingerprint of the IP address, to run the tables and limit abuse. Abandoned tables are deleted automatically; the game history is reset every Monday.
  • News: text and photos submitted by members are kept until they are deleted or a removal is requested.
  • Anonymous coinche deal log: every deal played by a human (solo or online) is recorded, to improve the computer players and to display totals on the coinche page: the cards, the bids and the result. No nickname, no message, no emoticon, no IP address, no date, no duration: a deal cannot be linked to anyone. This log is not published: only totals are (number of deals, share of contracts made or coinched…), never a deal; the oldest deals are erased beyond 300,000. Nothing is sent if your browser enables the Global Privacy Control signal.
  • Local storage: your seat at a table and a solo game may stay in the browser so that play can be resumed. Only the anonymous log above is sent from it.

Legal basis, recipients and retention

Processing is based on answering a request, performing the service requested, or the legitimate interest in security and operation. The data is accessible to authorized persons of the association and to its technical providers where strictly necessary, notably Cloudflare for hosting and protecting the site. Retention periods are those stated above or, failing that, the time needed for the purpose.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction, or object to a processing, where these rights apply. Write to scep.invaders.escp@gmail.com stating your request. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Third-party services

The links to Instagram and LinkedIn open services subject to their own policies. No social module or tracking script is loaded automatically on this site.
