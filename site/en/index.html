Video games, board games and tech projects

Whether you’re a LoL sweat, a casual-game fan or just curious, Scepi welcomes you with open arms for a game or two.

Video games

Internal Smash matches, a shared Minecraft server, a LoL tournament: at Scepi, we play all kinds of games.

Board games

Getting ganged up on 1v3 in Catan, coinche (a French card game) between classes (or during them ^^), intense chess games... If you’re less into video games, we also play plenty of board games together.

New technologies

Want to code your own video game? Become an AI pro? Build a really cool Scepi website? Scepi members will be there to support your projects.

Free online coinche and chess

Pick a game and jump in from your browser, on computer or phone. Nothing to install.
