Personal data
The site sets no advertising cookies and no audience-measurement cookies. It keeps only what its services need to work:
Online coinche: the nickname chosen and the seat taken, for the duration of the game; abandoned tables are deleted automatically. Anyone who opens a lobby or a private table leaves an encrypted fingerprint of their IP address (never the address itself), deleted along with the table, to limit the number of tables per person. Finished games (date, nicknames, scores) are kept in a history that is reset every Monday.
In your browser (local storage): your seat at a table and the solo game in progress, so that you can resume them after a reload. None of it is transmitted outside online games.
News: the articles (text and photos) published by the association’s members, until they are deleted.
The host, Cloudflare, processes visitors’ IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect the site, and may set a technical security cookie. For any question or request to access, rectify or delete your data: scep.invaders.escp@gmail.com. The links to Instagram and LinkedIn lead to third-party services subject to their own rules.
